About

Michael paints in his spare time. Most of his works begin as acrylic on canvas and then leave the flat surface: real branches, river stones, sand, a broken bottle or crumpled gold leaf become part of the picture.

His subjects move between nature, space and art history, from roots and canyons to the moon, an astronaut on a cliff edge and a nod to Mondrian. Each work on this site is shown with the date it was made and a few words about it.
